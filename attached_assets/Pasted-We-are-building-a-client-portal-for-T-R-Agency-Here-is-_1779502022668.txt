We are building a client portal for T/R Agency. Here is everything you need to build:

AUTH SYSTEM
Add login and create account page with four options: Google OAuth, Discord OAuth, GitHub OAuth, and email with password. Password rules are simple: minimum 8 characters, at least one capital letter, one lowercase letter, one number, nothing too strict. After creating an account or logging in they land on their portal dashboard.

AFTER FORM SUBMISSION FLOW
We already have a submission form and detail panel, keep that exactly as it is. After a client submits the form do not redirect them immediately. Instead show a message on screen saying something like "Your project has been initialized — check your portal to see your full project details and timeline" then after 5 seconds automatically redirect them to the Discord link.

THE CLIENT PORTAL DASHBOARD
This is the most important part, make it look premium and advanced like a real agency system. When a client logs in they should see:

A project card at the top showing a unique project ID in the format TR-2026-XXXX, the service they chose, the date and time they submitted, and a status badge that says ACTIVE — Team Notified.

Below that an activity timeline that auto-generates based on their submission timestamp showing steps like Project initialized, Request received by T/R Agency, Team notification sent, Awaiting Discord session, then greyed out future steps like Analysis begins and Report delivered. This should look live and automatic.

A stats row showing their number of active projects, the service price they paid, and the estimated delivery window for their chosen service.

A trust and security block at the bottom with lines like your project is private and encrypted, average first response under 2 hours, protected under T/R Agency client agreement.

A button or section that says Initialize Another Project that links back to the services section of the website so they can submit again.

If a client submitted a General Consultation request that should also appear in their portal the same way as any other project with its own card and timeline.

Make the whole portal look dark, sleek and high end. Think mission control not a basic dashboard. The client should open this and immediately feel like they hired a serious professional agency.

Store everything in a simple database, just the user info, service chosen, app details and timestamp. The visual complexity comes from how the data is displayed not from heavy backend logic.
